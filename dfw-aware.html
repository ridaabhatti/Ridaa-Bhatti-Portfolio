AWARE Skylink Northstar & Action Redesign

Connecting train, track, and station context with a clearer workflow for capturing actions, causes, and notes.

The problem

The AWARE Skylink initiative involved bringing together train, track, and station information while revisiting how operational actions, causes, and notes were captured. The product challenge was to connect system context with a workflow that people could understand and use.

My role

I contributed to discovery, wireframes, stakeholder reviews, and a demo for the Skylink Northstar and action redesign work. My focus was translating operational needs into an interface and capture workflow for review.

Discovery and workflow definition

The work considered trains, tracks, and stations alongside the action, cause, and notes experience. Wireframes made the proposed workflow visible so stakeholders could review it before moving further into implementation.

Product and technical decisions

The solution brought train, track, and station data into the product context and redesigned action, cause, and notes capture. The interface work connected operational information with the fields users needed to record their actions.

Review and demonstration

I took the proposed experience through reviews and a demo. These were opportunities to walk stakeholders through the workflow and discuss how it fit their operational needs.

Delivered output

Wireframes and a demonstrated redesign for the AWARE Skylink workflow. This portfolio does not imply a production rollout or a measured operational improvement.

Technical PM takeaway

Operational product work requires understanding how system entities relate to the user workflow. Visualizing that relationship helped bridge stakeholder conversations and the proposed technical experience.
