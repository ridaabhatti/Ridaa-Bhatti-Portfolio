NoorTab

A mindful new-tab experience combining daily reminders, focus tools, and Islamic content.

The problem

NoorTab explores how a frequently used browser surface can support a more intentional browsing experience. The product combines faith-based content and focus tools in a new-tab interface.

My role

I worked on the product direction and development of NoorTab. The project brought together defining the experience, organizing features, and building a Chrome extension.

The product experience

The portfolio prototype presents daily reminders, focus tools, and Islamic content within a mindful new-tab layout. Its central product question is how to make that content useful without overwhelming the browsing experience.

Technical context

NoorTab is a Chrome extension using Manifest V3. Browser extension development connects interface design with the browser environment, making this project an example of bringing a product concept into a technical format.
