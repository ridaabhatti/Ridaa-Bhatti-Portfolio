TPMO Project Health Dashboard

Turning monthly project reporting into a centralized Power BI view of project health, scope risk, and execution.

The problem

Monthly project reporting needed a centralized view that stakeholders could use to understand project health across airport technology initiatives. The product challenge was to make a broad portfolio readable while preserving the detail needed for individual projects.

My role

During my DFW internship, I contributed across discovery, stakeholder interviews, wireframing, requirements, and validation. I translated reporting needs into a dashboard structure and supported the path from requirements to deployment.

Discovery and requirements

I worked with stakeholders to understand their monthly reporting needs and used Visio wireframes to make the proposed experience concrete. PRDs and user stories helped connect those needs to the dashboard implementation.

Product and technical decisions

The dashboard used Project Advantage data in Power BI. Red, yellow, and green indicators covered Planning, Execution, Scope Risk, and Overall Health. Filters by Division, Project Manager, Fiscal Year, and Strategic Imperatives allowed users to move between portfolio context and relevant project subsets.

Validation and delivery

I prepared a QA plan and supported validation and deployment. This work connected the reporting requirements, dashboard experience, and checks needed before stakeholders could use the product.

Delivered output

A centralized project health dashboard with status indicators and stakeholder-relevant filters. This case study describes the delivered capability; no quantified time savings or adoption results are claimed.

Technical PM takeaway

A reporting product depends on both the data structure and the decisions it helps people make. This project connected stakeholder discovery, clear requirements, visual communication, and quality checks within one delivery lifecycle.
