TPMO Project Health Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard that brings monthly project health reporting into one place.

The problem

Airport teams needed a central place to review project health. The dashboard had to give them an overview of the portfolio and let them narrow it down to the projects relevant to their work.

My role

I helped take the dashboard from stakeholder conversations through requirements, wireframes, validation, and deployment during my DFW internship.

Understanding what teams needed

I spoke with stakeholders about their monthly reporting needs and used Visio wireframes to work through the layout. I documented requirements in a PRD and user stories so the proposed experience could guide implementation.

What went into the dashboard

The dashboard uses Project Advantage data in Power BI. It shows red, yellow, and green indicators for Planning, Execution, Scope Risk, and Overall Health. Users can filter by Division, Project Manager, Fiscal Year, and Strategic Imperatives.

Checking the work

I prepared a QA plan and supported validation before deployment. This helped connect the requirements we had gathered with what stakeholders would actually see and use.

The result

A centralized dashboard for reviewing project health, with status indicators and filters for different stakeholder needs.

What I took away

The chart or layout is only part of a reporting product. Understanding the questions people need answered is what makes the dashboard useful.

My focus

Discovery, requirements, wireframes, and QA
