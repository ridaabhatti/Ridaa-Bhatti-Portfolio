RSO Job Tracker Dashboard

Contributing KPI tiles and enhancements to an operational job-tracking dashboard.

The problem

The RSO Job Tracker initiative focused on enhancing an existing dashboard used for job tracking. The product opportunity was to give key information a clearer place in the experience.

My role

I contributed to KPI tiles and dashboard enhancements as one of three initiatives during my DFW internship. This was enhancement work within an existing product.

Product contribution

The work added emphasis to KPIs through tiles and improvements to the dashboard experience. The case study is limited to that contribution; specific KPI definitions and detailed implementation choices are not documented here.

Delivered output

KPI tile and dashboard enhancement contributions for the RSO Job Tracker. No quantified performance, usage, or efficiency claims are included.
